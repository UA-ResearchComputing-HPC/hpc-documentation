# User Publications

Our users do some really cool stuff! Explore a selection of recent research publications by University of Arizona researchers using the university's High Performance Computing resources. These publications highlight the breadth of research supported by HPC across disciplines and departments.

This is a curated selection, not a comprehensive record of all research conducted using University of Arizona HPC. If your work has benefited from our computing resources, we'd love to hear about it and have the opportunity to feature your publication here.

Have a publication to share? [Contact the HPC team](../../support_and_training/consulting_services/) to tell us about your work.
